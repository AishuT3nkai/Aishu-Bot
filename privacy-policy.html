A Aishu Bot Privacy Policy

AISHU COMMUNITY BOT

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Aishu Bot may process when it is used in a Discord server, why it is used, and what choices are available to members and server administrators.

Effective: October 5, 2026 Last updated: October 5, 2026

On this page

1. Scope 2. Information we process 3. How information is used 4. Sharing 5. Retention 6. Security 7. Your choices 8. Discord 9. Children and age 10. Changes 11. Contact

01

Scope

Aishu Bot is a Discord community bot for moderation, verification, member profiles, server management, utility features, leveling, economy, tickets, suggestions, reports, and related community tools.

This policy applies to information processed by Aishu through its Discord bot features and its local/server-side storage. It does not replace Discord's own privacy policy or terms.

02

Information we process

Aishu is designed to process only information needed for enabled features. Depending on how a server uses the bot, this can include:

Member-provided information

Information submitted through features such as introductions and birthdays, including a name, age, date-of-birth information, origin, city, and birthday month/day.

Discord information

Discord identifiers and public account/server metadata needed to identify members, display profiles, perform moderation, verification, permissions, and server features.

Server configuration

Settings such as roles, channels, automod rules, anti-raid settings, verification settings, welcome/goodbye settings, ticket configuration, role panels, reaction-role mappings, and related preferences.

Community activity

Information needed for enabled suggestions, reports, tickets, moderation cases, leveling/economy, and security features. Some security and backup features may store relevant message or event information.

Not requested by Aishu: Aishu does not ask users for Discord passwords, bot tokens, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or Discord account credentials.

03

How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and personalize Aishu's community features.
  • Store member profiles and server preferences requested by users or administrators.
  • Run moderation, verification, anti-spam, anti-raid, and security controls.
  • Maintain tickets, suggestions, reports, moderation cases, leveling, and economy features when enabled.
  • Produce server statistics and operational information needed by the bot.
  • Protect the bot, servers, and members from abuse or unauthorized actions.

Aishu does not use member data for advertising or sell personal information.

04

Sharing

Aishu does not sell personal information. Information may be visible to server administrators, moderators, or other members when a Discord feature is intentionally designed to make it visible.

For example, configured reports and suggestions may be routed to staff channels, and moderation logs may contain information about moderation actions. Aishu may also rely on Discord infrastructure to provide the underlying platform and API functionality.

05

Retention

Retention depends on the feature and the server's configuration. Persistent bot configuration and feature data can remain in Aishu's database until it is changed or removed. Temporary rate-limit and activity tracking may be held only for as long as needed by the feature.

Some backup and disaster-recovery features may retain snapshots or recent message/embed information for recovery purposes. Such information is not guaranteed to reproduce every original Discord message or attachment.

When a server no longer needs a feature, administrators should disable or remove that feature and request removal of stored data through the contact route described below where applicable.

06

Security

Aishu uses Discord permissions and role-hierarchy checks for sensitive moderation actions. Security-related configuration is stored server-side, and secrets such as the Discord bot token are intended to remain in environment variables rather than source code.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. If a security issue is discovered, it should be reported to the bot administrators promptly.

07

Your choices

  • Do not provide optional profile information: You can choose not to use features that ask for member-provided information.
  • Update information: Where a feature provides an update or removal command, use that command to change your stored information.
  • Server controls: Server administrators can disable configured Aishu features or remove the bot from their server.
  • Data requests: If you need information reviewed or removed and no command is available, contact the administrators of the Discord server where Aishu is installed.

08

Discord and third-party services

Aishu operates through Discord. Discord independently controls its own platform, infrastructure, account systems, and data practices. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's terms and privacy documentation.

Aishu may use Discord's API and gateway to receive the events and metadata required for enabled bot functions. Aishu does not control information that Discord processes independently of the bot.

09

Children and age

Aishu is a Discord bot and does not independently verify a user's age. Discord has its own age requirements and privacy controls. Do not submit information to Aishu that you are not permitted to share under the rules applicable to your Discord account or local law.

10

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Aishu's features, data handling, or legal requirements change. The effective and last-updated dates at the top of this page will be changed when a material revision is published.

11

Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or security concerns, contact the administrators responsible for the Discord server where Aishu Bot is installed. Include enough information to identify the relevant server and feature, but do not send passwords, tokens, or other secrets.
